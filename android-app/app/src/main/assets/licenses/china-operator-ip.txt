china-operator-ip (https://github.com/gaoyifan/china-operator-ip)

Used by Hetu for the CNIP lists: the built-in snapshots assets/cnip/hetu-cn-v4.txt and
assets/cnip/hetu-cn-v6.txt, and the daily updates of ip-lists/china.txt and ip-lists/china6.txt
that the core rule-providers download. Hetu imports the same lists into the hetu_cn4 / hetu_cn6
ipsets for the kernel-level CNIP bypass.
